##### Hohenberg-Mermin-Wagner-Coleman theorem

- N. D. Mermin and H. Wagner, *Absence of Ferromagnetism or Antiferromagnetism in One- or Two-Dimensional Isotropic Heisenberg Models*, [Phys. Rev. Lett. **17**, 1133 (1966)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.17.1133).

- S. Coleman, *There are no Goldstone bosons in two dimensions*, [Commun. Math. Phys. **31**, 259 (1973)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01646487).

Kosterlitz, *The Critical Properties of the Two-Dimensional XY Model*, [J. Phys. C **7**, 1046 (1974)](https://doi.org/10.1088/0022-3719/7/6/005).
